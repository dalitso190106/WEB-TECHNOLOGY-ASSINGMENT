About Me

Hey there! I am Dalitso Emmanuel Phiri, currently in my second year studying Computer Science at Mulungushi University. I first got into programming because I became curious about how the sites we use daily are built behind the scenes. Knowing that a few lines of code can bring a whole digital experience to life made me want to build things myself. Web tech feels like a very good combination of problem-solving and creative design. Through this course, I am looking forward to building a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with the goal of launching a personal portfolio site I am genuinely proud to share with future employers.
